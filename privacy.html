Privacy

Booth · effective August 5, 2026

Booth helps you find analog photobooths and keep a record of the ones you visit. This page says exactly what the app stores and why. There are no ads, no trackers, and your data is never sold.

What Booth stores

Your phone number. Signing in is passwordless: we text you a code. Your number is used to sign you in and never for marketing. Codes are delivered by Twilio, which processes your number solely to send that text.

Your profile. A display name and a booth color, chosen by you.

Your saves and strips. Booths you save, and photo strips you add — the photo, which booth it came from, the date, and an optional caption. Strips are your private record; Booth has no feed, no followers, and no public profiles.

Where it lives

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Strip photos also keep a copy on your device so your library works offline.

Location

The map opens on the city you choose. If a locate-me feature asks for your location in a future version, your position will be read to place you on the map and will not be stored on our servers.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account, inside the app. This permanently deletes your profile, saves, strips, and photos, and the account itself. There is no undo.
